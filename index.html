Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DodgeDrop.

Do I need to install any app or APK on my Android phone?

No! That is the core beauty of DodgeDrop. Modern Android phones (Android 6 and newer) already have Google Quick Share built into their operating system. You simply select photos or files, tap Share ➔ Quick Share, and choose your PC.

Does DodgeDrop require an active internet connection?

No. DodgeDrop operates purely over your local home or office Wi-Fi / Ethernet router. Your data stays entirely within your four walls. It works even during an internet outage.

Are photos or 4K videos compressed during transfer?

Never. Unlike messaging apps that downscale and compress your media, DodgeDrop transfers exact bit-for-bit files. RAW camera photos, ProRes recordings, and huge archives remain completely untouched.

Can I transfer files to an iPhone, iPad, or Mac?

Yes! For Apple devices or other computers, DodgeDrop provides Option 2: scan the QR code on your desktop screen with your iPhone camera to instantly open the DodgeDrop Web Portal in Safari. You can upload and download files at top Wi-Fi speeds without installing any software.

Why didn't my phone see my PC initially?

Ensure both your PC and phone are connected to the same Wi-Fi router. On your Android phone, set Quick Share visibility to "Everyone" (or tap 'Turn on for 10 minutes'). This instructs your phone to immediately discover local Wi-Fi devices.
